Gravity Duel v0.12.0

30 unique solo arenas: 7 + 10 + 13.
Each unit retains four-way free-for-all and 1v2, then adds a separate boss.
Multiplayer retains 2v2 and four-way competition, with no bosses.
No weapon upgrades. Higher-resolution rendering. Phone dual sticks: release right stick to fire; center to cancel.

Public solo play: https://herald1921.github.io/gravity-duel/
Old version: https://herald1921.github.io/gravity-duel/legacy-v0.11.1/

Online client/server are locally tested; public online needs a deployed host and online.json endpoint.
See README for deployment and testing limitations.
